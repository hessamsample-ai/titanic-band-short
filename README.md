## 5. Safe Wording & Fact-Check

### Confirmed Historical Facts:
- All eight musicians were aboard RMS Titanic on April 14–15, 1912.
- The band played on the boat deck during the sinking.
- The exact final song is **debated** (commonly cited: *"Nearer, My God, to Thee"* or *"Songe d'Automne"*). We will **not** state one as absolute fact.
- All eight perished. Hartley's body was recovered and identified. Others were not all recovered.
- They were employed by a music agency (C.W. & F.N. Black), not directly by White Star Line.

### Safe Wording Rules:
| ✅ USE | ❌ AVOID |
|--------|----------|
| "are remembered for staying at their posts" | "died screaming / drowned in agony" |
| "their final performance became symbolic" | "they played while people died around them" |
| "did not leave their posts" | "sacrificed themselves heroically" (implies certainty of conscious choice we can't verify) |
| "the story became part of the Titanic's memory" | "the greatest act of bravery in history" |
| "the last song is debated, but the act is not" | stating one specific song as confirmed fact |

### Tone Rule:
We honor their memory. We do not exploit their death. No sensationalism. No horror framing. No "you won't believe what happened" energy.
